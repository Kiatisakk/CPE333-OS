Without \texttt{static}, \texttt{y} becomes an automatic variable allocated on
the stack. It is reinitialised to 5 upon entering each loop iteration and
deallocated upon exiting, yielding the value 6 on every pass. The address falls
within the high stack range (\texttt{0x7ff\ldots}); it remains identical across
iterations due to stack slot reuse rather than state persistence.
