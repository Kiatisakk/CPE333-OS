--- 1. run it, then press CTRL+Z ---
$ ./ss1_2.sh
ss1_2.sh (pid 1468): started, now sleeping 1000 seconds
^Z

--- 2. what CTRL+Z did ---
$ jobs
[1]+  Stopped                    ./ss1_2.sh
$ ps -o pid,stat,args -p 1468
    PID STAT COMMAND
   1468 T    bash ./ss1_2.sh

--- 3. bg: resume it, in the background ---
$ bg %1
[1]+ ./ss1_2.sh &
$ jobs
[1]+  Running                    ./ss1_2.sh &
$ ps -o pid,stat,args -p 1468
    PID STAT COMMAND
   1468 S    bash ./ss1_2.sh

--- 4. fg: bring it back to the foreground ---
$ fg %1
./ss1_2.sh
Terminated                 ./ss1_2.sh

$ jobs
