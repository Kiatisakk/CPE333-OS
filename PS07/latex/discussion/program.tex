The example is a concert box office rather than the lecture's shared counter.
A hall of one million seats is sold by several booking threads that share one
variable, \texttt{seats\_left}. To sell a ticket a thread checks that a seat is
free and then writes back one fewer; nothing makes that pair of steps
indivisible. Each thread counts its own sales in a private variable, so the
total sold is exact and any excess over one million is tickets sold for seats
that do not exist.

The number of threads comes from the first argument. With a second argument,
\texttt{yield}, the decrement is split into the explicit load, decrement,
\texttt{sched\_yield()} and store of the sheet's example, forcing a context
switch between reading the counter and writing it back.
