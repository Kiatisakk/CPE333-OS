Forcing a context switch makes the overselling larger on average, and the
totals still differ from run to run. A thread now holds its private copy of the
counter across the \texttt{sched\_yield()} while the other threads keep
selling. When it resumes it stores its stale value, putting back every seat
they sold in the meantime, so those seats are sold again.
